## claim-0009 [historiographic_claim/high] span=2280-2282 cand=2004-gaofeng-zhongguo-shejishi:cand-03-14
ZH: 高丰把中国真正瓷器的发明时间定在东汉，书中叙述为东汉晚期；理由是已发现一批有确凿年代可考的东汉青瓷器，浙江上虞窑址最多，上虞小仙坛窑青瓷标本经中国科学院硅酸盐研究所测试符合近代瓷标准。
EN: Gao Feng dates the invention of true porcelain to the Eastern Han, described in the book as the late Eastern Han, citing dated Eastern Han celadons, most numerously from Shangyu kiln sites in Zhejiang, and tests of Xiaoxiantan celadon sherds by the Chinese Academy of Sciences' Institute of Silicate Research that met modern porcelain standards.
NOTE: L2280 述商代中期原始瓷至东汉晚期经一千多年探索而发明真正瓷器；L2282 明言把瓷器发明时间定在东汉并给出上虞小仙坛窑测试依据。

## claim-0011 [interpretive_claim/medium] span=1899-1899 cand=2004-gaofeng-zhongguo-shejishi:cand-03-20
ZH: 高丰引雷圭元之说，汉代铜镜图案属四方八位的米字格装饰格律体，风格规矩有余、活泼不足，静止性多、飞动性少；并据此判断汉镜装饰构成体现中国古代图案装饰设计的一种典型民族风格。
EN: Gao Feng, following Lei Guiyuan's characterization of Han mirror patterns as a four-direction, eight-position mi-grid 'decorative metrical style' that is orderly but static, concludes that Han mirror composition embodies a typical national style of Chinese decorative design.

## claim-0017 [historiographic_claim/high] span=4152-4152 cand=2004-gaofeng-zhongguo-shejishi:cand-06-16
ZH: 高丰认为，从艺术设计史角度看，明代而非清代康雍乾是中国古代瓷器的高峰。理由有三：明代官窑与民窑青花均达很高水平且清代未超过；明代瓷器造型种类丰富并实现成套组合的系列化设计突破；明代解决了釉上彩绘这一瓷器装饰的最大难题。
EN: Gao Feng argues that in design-historical terms the Ming, not the Kangxi-Yongzheng-Qianlong era of the Qing, is the peak of Chinese porcelain: Ming official and popular blue-and-white reached a level not surpassed in the Qing; Ming forms were rich and achieved serialized sets; and Ming potters solved the major problem of overglaze painting.
NOTE: 断言语句集中在 L4152，明确列出三条理由；候选跨度 L4150-L4155 含小节标题，取 L4152 为关键行。

## claim-0020 [interpretive_claim/high] span=4660-4675 cand=2004-gaofeng-zhongguo-shejishi:cand-06-19
ZH: 高丰认为，宋体字经元代与明初反复摸索，于明代中叶之后定型为四边见方、横细直粗、字体端正、整齐大方的标准印刷字体；其成熟定型是中国古代平面设计史上一个重要的成就和重大突破，其点画组合兼具楷书审美韵味与装饰化、程式化的美学特征。
EN: Gao Feng argues that the Songti typeface, after experiments in the Yuan and early Ming, was standardized by the mid-Ming as a regular, square, thin-horizontal and thick-vertical printing type; its maturation was a major achievement in Chinese graphic-design history, combining the aesthetic flavour of regular script with decorative, formulaic qualities.
NOTE: L4660 述宋体字在明代中叶后定型及其结构、风格特点；L4675 从雕版印刷与平面设计两个角度评价其意义。候选跨度 L4652-L4687 为包围跨度。

## claim-0036 [factual_claim/medium] span=5219-5219 cand=2004-gaofeng-zhongguo-shejishi:cand-sup-76
