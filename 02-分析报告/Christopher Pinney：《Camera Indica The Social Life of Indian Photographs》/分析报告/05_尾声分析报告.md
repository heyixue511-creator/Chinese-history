# 05_尾声分析报告：Epilogue

---

## L### 一、章节定位与功能

### L### 1.1 在全书结构中的位置

Epilogue（尾声）是全书正文的最后部分，以日记体田野笔记的形式呈现（"17/7/92: Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi"），记录了Pinney在Delhi旧城Chandni Chowk街头摄影师中的一天经历。它在功能上既是对第3章的延伸（同样的montage逻辑从Nagda室内延伸到Delhi街头），也是全书的**隐喻性收束**。

### L### 1.2 功能定位

- **空间拓展**：从Nagda（区域工业城镇）→Chandni Chowk（国家首都的历史核心）——摄影实践的地理跨度隐喻着全书的主题范围。
- **三个"moment"的物理共存**：在Chandni Chowk的同一个街角，passport photos（第1章的colonial surveillance的后代）、film star montages（第2章的élite portraiture的大众化延续）、和composite prints（第3章的Nagda montage的都市版本）并置在同一空间中。
- **作者位置的转变**：Pinney从观察者变为参与者——他让Amar Jeet制作了一张他与Madhuri Dixit的"婚礼蒙太奇"——这是全书最具自反性的时刻。
- **理论总结的替代**：Epilogue不提供传统的"结论"，而是以一个视觉意象（"multiple-eyed sitter"的composite print, illus. 126）作为全书的收束——这个图像本身即是"a metaphor for the complexity that living in post-colonial India demands"。

## L### 二、结构分析

### L### 2.1 结构线索

| 段落 | 内容 |
|------|------|
| **第1段** | 场景设定：Chandni Chowk街头，约8个摄影师，木制大画幅相机，相框中的passport照片 |
| **第2段** | Film star montages：同一群人的面孔被"切割"进与Amir Khan, Jackie Shroff, Dharmendra等明星的合照 |
| **第3段** | Amar Jeet的个人故事（家族来自Pakistan，三代从事此业）；Pinney参与制作与Madhuri Dixit的"结婚"蒙太奇 |
| **第4段** | Multiple-eyed sitter的composite print（illus. 126）——"the answer to a puzzle" |
| **第5段** | Kishangarh的"Versailles gone berserk"画作（illus. 127）——18世纪Rajput绘画中殖民视觉hybridization的早期踪迹 |
| **第6段** | 理论收束：Chandni Chowk体现了"the fall-out from enduring traditions of image-making, the engagement with and attempt to negate colonial encounters, and the bureaucratic needs of the state"三者的共存 |

### L### 2.2 结构特征

Epilogue采用**dense montage**的写作结构——场景描写、个人轶事、理论反思、艺术史引文被压缩进约1000词的空间中——模拟了它所描述的Chandni Chowk摄影师使用的"cutting"技术。

## L### 三、内容分析（核心论题+关键论点案例）

### L### 3.1 核心论题

**L### 3.1.1 总论题：post-colonial visual complex（后殖民视觉综合体）**

Chandni Chowk的街头摄影摊档同时呈现了全书的三个"moments"：
- **Passport photos**（"the hopeful, the anxious, the suspicious, the quizzical"）——延续了第1章中殖民国家身份识别技术的谱系。但在这里，它们服务于individual aspirations（工作申请、签证、贷款）而非state surveillance。
- **Film star montages**——延续了第2章élite portraiture和第3章Nagda "dream chambers"的逻辑。普通人通过"cutting"将自己插入与Bollywood明星的亲密关系中。
- **Composite prints with multiple viewpoints**（illus. 126）——延续了第3章的"dividual" person概念和Indian cosmology of multiple worlds。

> L###："In Chandni Chowk the fall-out from enduring traditions of image-making, the engagement with and attempt to negate colonial encounters, and the bureaucratic needs of the state all seemed to jostle together in the same space."

**L### 3.1.2 "this Chandni Chowk Rodchenko"**

Pinney将自己遇到的一位Chandni Chowk摄影师称为"this Chandni Chowk Rodchenko"——俄罗斯构成主义艺术家Alexander Rodchenko以激进摄影角度和蒙太奇闻名。这个称呼暗示：
- **现代主义视觉语言的全球旅行**：Rodchenko的蒙太奇美学可能经由某些不可追踪的路径进入了Delhi街头摄影师的实践
- **视觉创造的平行发生**：也可能是Chandni Chowk摄影师独立发展出与Rodchenko类似的美学策略

### L### 3.2 关键案例

**L### 3.2.1 Amar Jeet的Madhuri Dixit蒙太奇**

> L###："Amar Jeet, whose family had come from Pakistan and who had been in the business for the last three generations, made a double composite portrait and then I asked him to do a marriage shot of myself with Madhuri Dixit ... This is the most potent magic I have ever seen, all accomplished on a street corner with a wooden camera and an old bucket, and I am left with a photographic trace of my intimate relationship with Madhuri."

这是全书最具方法论自反性的段落：
- Pinney从一个分析者变为被分析的参与者——他自己成为蒙太奇的subject
- 技术描述：Madhuri的明信片被"photographed upside-down"→Pinney被拍摄→两张paper negatives被"carefully cut up with large scissors"→重新拍摄→positive print
- "potent magic"——Pinney承认这种简单的技术产生了真实的情感效果："I am left with a photographic trace of my intimate relationship with Madhuri"
- "a large crowd gathered and were entertained"——摄影实践是公共的、社交的事件

**L### 3.2.2 Multiple-eyed sitter (illus. 126)**

> L###："Then, out of the corner of my eye, I see a remarkable composite print of a multiple-eyed sitter, poised between two different viewpoints. On either side of the combined print are the two original images, each respecting an eyewitness principle."

这张图像的解读是全书"结论"的替代：
- 两个"eyewitness principle"的图像（Gombrich）被组合成一个multiple-perspective的composite
- Pinney联想到Markandeya在Vishnu口中滑进滑出（第3章）
- 但这个图像不仅仅是对印度宇宙论的"传统"引用，因为"above the image was a videshi (foreign) sitter, smiling genially"——"Here was no mere traditionalism"

**L### 3.2.3 Kishangarh的"Versailles gone berserk" (illus. 127)**

> L###："This striking trace of the hybridization of schemata that occurs within colonialism was once described by Stuart Cary Welch as 'Versailles gone berserk'."

Pinney在Epilogue中引入18世纪Rajput绘画Vistas at Kishangarh——这是全书时间跨度最远的图像参照。这一引入将Chandni Chowk的当代摄影实践置于一个长时段的colonial visual hybridization谱系中。

## L### 四、逻辑梳理（论证链条+因果转折）

### L### 4.1 论证链

```
场景设定（Chandni Chowk 17/7/92）
    ↓
三种图像的并置观察（passport / star montage / composite）
    ↓
参与体验（Madhuri Dixit wedding montage）
    ↓
发现multiple-eyed sitter composite
    ↓
联想：Markandeya + videshi sitter → "no mere traditionalism"
    ↓
关联：Kishangarh painting (18th c.) → longue durée of colonial visual hybridization
    ↓
总结："the outcomes of liaisons with artifacts cannot be predicted"（Nicholas Thomas）
```

### L### 4.2 作为"反结论"的Epilogue

传统的学术结论会提供总结、概括和封闭。Pinney的Epilogue提供的是**开放、并置和新的困惑**：
- 不是"these three moments show that..."，而是"this image ... was the answer to a puzzle that had lain in my head for many years"——答案本身是一个新的谜题
- 不是closure，而是"the outcomes of liaisons with artifacts cannot be predicted"——未来是不可预测的

### L### 5.2 材料使用特色

- **日记体直接引用**："17/7/92: Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi:"——呈现田野遭遇的原始质感
- **"I see"作为方法论**：Epilogue多次使用视觉发现的措辞——"out of the corner of my eye, I see"——强调民族志知识的偶然性和视觉性
- **时序跳跃**：18世纪绘画→1992年街头→个人的长期困惑——时间的折叠本身就是Epilogue的"montage"方法

## L### 六、论辩与阐述方法

### L### 6.1 核心论辩策略

**L### 6.1.1 以图像替代论述**

Epilogue的核心阐述策略是**展示而非论证**——illus. 126（multiple-eyed sitter）被呈现为一个"答案"，但它本身就是一个需要解读的图像谜题。Pinney通过呈现这个图像，而不是通过对它的解释性文字，来传达全书的最终立场。

**L### 6.1.2 "jostle together"的空间隐喻**

> L###："all seemed to jostle together in the same space"

"Jostle"（推搡、拥挤）——这个动词暗示了三种视觉逻辑之间的关系不是和谐的"综合"（synthesis），而是紧张的、身体性的共存。这避免了任何关于"后殖民综合体"过于整洁的叙述。

**L### 6.1.3 "I thought of"的联想方法**

> L###："I thought of Markandeya slipping in and out of the mouth of the god"

联想（association）而非论证（argumentation）——这是Epilogue的认识论。通过图像→神话→绘画→困惑的联想链，Pinney暗示了理解这种视觉实践需要的方法不是线性推理，而是跨时空的自由联想。

## L### 七、语言文风（原文摘录+L###）

### L### 7.1 原文摘录

> L### "17/7/92: Chandni Chowk, Old Delhi: on the corner, where this busy road faces the Red Fort, are about eight photographers working with large home-made box cameras."

> L### "How many crucial moments in all these individual lives are collected here on public display – all those prospective loans, jobs in the Gulf, jobs nearer home, train season tickets that mark the possession of a job? These photographs are flickering across the desperate and uncertain space of contemporary urban India."

> L### "Gone is the weary, deadened look on the face of the ex-student applying for his hundredth job, banished is the exhausted countenance of the rickshaw wallah seeking a loan to liberate himself from high rents charged by the syndicate that owns his vehicle. These same faces, fractured by the clumsy cutting of the photographers, now sit astride horses clutching guns."

> L### "This is the most potent magic I have ever seen, all accomplished on a street corner with a wooden camera and an old bucket, and I am left with a photographic trace of my intimate relationship with Madhuri."

> L### "Then, out of the corner of my eye, I see a remarkable composite print of a multiple-eyed sitter, poised between two different viewpoints ... I thought of Markandeya slipping in and out of the mouth of the god, but the wider setting of the image forced on me the awareness that this was not simply the eruption of the past into this bustling Delhi thoroughfare."

> L### "Here was no mere traditionalism, but a metaphor for the complexity that living in post-colonial India demands."

> L### "This striking trace of the hybridization of schemata that occurs within colonialism was once described by Stuart Cary Welch as 'Versailles gone berserk'."

> L### "This image, and those around it in Chandni Chowk, epitomize the manner in which the 'concrete historical circulation' of images reveals that 'the outcomes of liaisons with artifacts cannot be predicted'."

> L### "The imagination of a Chandni Chowk photographer captured the mobility and inventiveness of many contemporary Indian identities, fragments of whose archaeology I have attempted to present in this book."

### L### 7.2 文风特征

1. **日记体的即时性**：以日期开篇——"17/7/92"——创造了"现场直击"的文本效果。这种文体选择将Epilogue与前面三章更正式的学术散文区分开来。
2. **抒情性与政治性的融合**：passport photos的描写——"the hopeful, the anxious, the suspicious, the quizzical"——在四组形容词中将个体的多样性压缩为一系列情感状态的并置。"How many crucial moments in all these individual lives are collected here on public display?"——这个反问句将passport photos从官僚技术提升为人类命运的索引。
3. **"flickering"意象的回归**：第1章开头Tagg的"flickering across a field of institutional spaces"在第1章结尾和Epilogue中被重复——"These photographs are flickering across the desperate and uncertain space of contemporary urban India."——这个呼应为全书提供了一个意象上的闭合。
4. **"fragments of whose archaeology"**：最后一句话——"fragments of whose archaeology I have attempted to present in this book"——回应了Preface中的"fragments"方法论。全书本身被呈现为一个"考古学"——不是完整的重建，而是碎片的挖掘和并置。
5. **末尾的未完成感**：正文结束于"all seemed to jostle together in the same space."——这个ending是有意未完成的。它不是结论性的period，而是开放性的ellipsis。

## L### 九、与前后章关联

### L### 9.1 与第3章的关联

- **直接延续**：第3章的Nagda studio montage（Suhag, Sagar）→Chandni Chowk街头montage——同一种视觉逻辑从室内延伸到街头
- **第3章末尾的呼应**：第3章以H. R. Raja的Chandra Shekhar Azad & Bhagat Singh日历图像结尾——"the face in contemporary Indian representations can be erased of its physiognomy"——Epilogue中同一个逻辑被推进到"multiple-eyed sitter"
- **"flickering"的复现**：第1章开头Tagg的"flickering"在Epilogue中再次出现——"These photographs are flickering across the desperate and uncertain space of contemporary urban India"

### L### 9.2 与第1章的关联

- **Passport photos的殖民前史**：Epilogue中的passport photos可以追溯到第1章中Bengal Police的measurement roll card、Bertillon系统、Herschel的指纹术——身份识别技术从colonial surveillance到post-colonial aspiration的转化
- **"flickering"的三次出现**：第1章开头（Tagg引文）、第1章结尾（Pinney归纳）、Epilogue（passport photos描写）——这个意象形成全书的主题性循环

### L### 9.3 与第2章的关联

- **Film star montages与swagger portrait**：第2章Batoni的贵族swagger portrait→Epilogue中普通人将自己插入与Bollywood明星的montage——"swagger"从阶级特权转化为大众可购买的摄影服务
- **Phalke的magic与Amar Jeet的magic**：第2章Phalke的"ghost in the camera"→Epilogue中Pinney的"most potent magic I have ever seen"——magical transformation的谱系

### L### 9.4 与Preface的关联

- **"fragments"方法论的完成**：Preface中承诺的"deployment of these various fragments"→Epilogue中的"fragments of whose archaeology"——全书的方法论框架在此闭合
- **"concrete historical circulation"的例示**：Preface引用Appadurai的概念→Epilogue展示了Chandni Chowk如何"epitomize"这一概念
