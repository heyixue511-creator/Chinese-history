# 03_第二章分析报告：Indian Eyes

---

## L### 一、章节定位与功能

### L### 1.1 在全书结构中的位置

第二章是全书三个"moments"中的**第二个moment**，与第1章覆盖大致相同的历史时期（约1850s至1920s），但聚焦于与殖民国家监控完全不同的摄影实践领域——印度本土精英的摄影创作。标题"Indian Eyes"直接取自Judith Mara Gutman的著作Through Indian Eyes（1982），但Pinney将其转化为一个反本质主义的分析。

### L### 1.2 功能定位

- **提供第1章的"另一面"**：在第1章的殖民监控（anxiety, classification, indexicality）之外，呈现印度精英在同一时期如何创造性地使用摄影——从模仿欧洲"swagger portrait"到创造独特的宫廷和自我表征策略。
- **与Gutman对话并批判**：将Gutman作为"负面参照点"——她的"Indian-ness"文化本质主义论点被反复引用、部分肯定、最终拒绝——Pinney坚持"the field of power around the camera rather than cultural practice"是首要分析维度。
- **追踪从"宫廷摄影"到"大众视觉文化"的萌芽**：通过Lala Deen Dayal→D. G. Phalke→Ravi Varma→chromolithographs的链条，揭示19世纪末20世纪初多重视觉媒介（摄影、绘画、电影、chromolithography）开始互动交融的关键历史时刻。
- **记录Nara Ratna Mandir的失败**：Arundale的"人类伟大之庙"是Victorian moral portraiture与印度darshan传统之间不可调和的混合——其失败揭示了不同"肖像意识形态"之间的根本张力。

### L### 1.3 与第1章的关系

第1章的"field of power"是国家监控和知识生产；第2章的"field of power"是宫廷赞助、商业竞争和民族主义政治。Pinney主张真正决定照片性质差异的不是"Indian vs. Western"文化特性（Gutman），而是"official vs. personal photography"的权力场域差异。

## L### 三、内容分析（核心论题+关键论点案例）

### L### 3.1 核心论题

**L### 3.1.1 总论题：photography as theatrical augmentation（作为剧场性增补的摄影）**

第2章的核心论点是：在印度精英手中，摄影不是"纪录"或"分类"的工具（如第1章），而是**person-building（人格建构）**的技术。Lalbag宫殿的建筑和Ramchandra Rao的摄影被描述为：

> L### "parallel technologies of augmentation, both working to leave substantive traces of what otherwise would be mere dreams."

**L### 3.1.2 子论题1：painted photographs的符号学意义**

印度摄影师大量使用涂料覆盖照片——在欧美符号学体系中，这是一种"污染"（将低级的iconic sign施加于高级的indexical sign），但在印度实践中，涂料不是缺乏而是"semiotic democracy"（符号学民主）的体现。Pinney引用Gutman的观察：

> L### "painting on photographs suggests a kind of impurity – on the one hand an excuse for failing to reach more perfect heights, on the other, a device for watering down what should be a purely photographic statement."

但Pinney将这一观察从其本质主义框架中剥离——这不是"Indian mind"的表达，而是特定历史条件下商业实践和美学选择的产物。

**L### 3.1.3 子论题2：Gutman批判——"Indian-ness" vs. "field of power"**

Pinney对Gutman的批判有三个层次：
1. **方法论层面**：Gutman假定了一种超历史的"Indian conception of reality"，忽视了"dramatic changes in patronage structures and the public sphere"。
2. **经验层面**：许多"Indian"工作室服务的是欧洲客户；许多工作室有混合所有权；Indian photographers完全能参与ethnological surveillance——"Indian photographers were just as capable as Europeans of participating in the project of ethnological surveillance"。
3. **替代框架**：不是"Indian vs. Western"，而是"official vs. personal photography"——"what differentiates the images ... is, first and foremost, the field of power around the camera rather than cultural practice"。

**L### 3.1.4 子论题3：Nara Ratna Mandir的两种肖像意识形态**

Arundale的"人类伟大之庙"试图融合两种不可兼容的肖像理论：
- **Darshan**（接触神圣）：图像的力量来自与神圣的物理邻近——"physical visual 'contact' with the image"。图像不需要呈现具体的个人品格。
- **Victorian moral portraiture**（Palmerston）：面孔自身承载可读的道德品质——"to see before them the features of those who have done things which are worthy of our admiration"。

两者的杂交产生了"something hybrid and confused"——这是Nara Ratna Mandir失败（年访问量仅1000-2000人）的深层原因。

### L### 3.2 关键案例

**L### 3.2.1 Parsi工作室的"artist-photographer"自我呈现**

EOS Photographic Company的cabinet cards上刻有画家调色板、画笔与相机的组合图案——"representing photography as a sort of painting"（L###）。这与第1章中殖民官方对索引性的强调形成鲜明对照。艺术家的idiom比技术员的idiom更适合商业竞争——"the apparatus of the painter's studio was easily adopted ... the idiom of art and the persona of the artist – replete as they were with the transcendent and scarce – were much more amenable to commercial manipulation than those of the technician"。

**L### 3.2.2 Maharaja Sawai Ram Singh II & T. Murray的yogic肖像**

> L### "Ram Singh adopts the persona of a devout and simple worshipper of the gods. He wears only a dhoti and various ritually powerful necklaces, and sits on a mat surrounded by the paraphernalia of worship ... There is a quiet, intimate theatricality in this image of a pure and simple king attending to his otherworldly duties."

这张照片是"a joint communiqué about the nature of an idealized ruler"——国王和英国摄影师共同创造了这一图像。摄影在这里不是"impose a category of identity plucked from a preexisting structure"，而是"a creative space in which new aspirant identities and personae can be conjured"。

**L### 3.2.3 Ramchandra Rao & Pratap Rao的Holkar王室摄影**

三张Indore宫廷照片展示了摄影的多种"面具"功能：
- **Shivaji Rao flying**（illus. 49）：国王站在早期单翼飞机前——飞行背景是欧洲博览会摄影的常见套路，但在宫廷语境中获得新的意义。
- **Shivaji Rao rowing**（illus. 50）：国王在"decidedly European-looking river"上划船。
- **Yashwantrao I的等身像**（c. 1917）：传统的正式王室肖像。

这三张照片展示了Holkar王室使用摄影的灵活性——"to project dignified formal representations of royal grandeur"和"to exploit its ability to create dreamworlds"。

**L### 3.2.4 Phalke的"相机驱鬼"轶事**

> L### "Phalke prepared a painted negative depicting the sort of monstrous ghost from which the agonized prince was suffering. He then seated the prince under a tree and photographed him, as he had wished to do all along."

这个轶事完美体现了Phalke的摄影观——与Ravi Varma的"mimetic precision"（Chitralekha寓言）不同，Phalke的摄影"opens up a new space of magical conjunctions"。两个故事——Chitralekha以精确肖像帮助Usha识别梦中情人 vs. Phalke以伪造的鬼魂底片"治愈"王子——代表了两种截然不同的视觉实践理念：mimesis vs. creative intervention。

**L### 3.2.5 Representative Men of India中的Dadabhai Naoroji（1889）**

> L### "The most cursory examination of it will suffice to show how altogether inaccurately, through the casual use of a colloquialism, Lord Salisbury applied to the owner of so Caucasian a head the descriptive phrase of 'black man'!"（George Birdwood）

Birdwood的评论是全书最具张力的段落之一——殖民人类学建立的种族分类学在此被"友谊"的纽带所瓦解。Naoroji的"Caucasian head"（通过照片"证明"）使得Salisbury的"black man"称谓显得荒谬。照片在此不是分类/区隔的工具，而是跨越种族边界的"证据"。

**L### 3.2.6 Arundale的Nara Ratna Mandir（1923）**

这个"人类伟大之庙"的肖像画廊包括：Rama, Guru Nanak, Mohammed, Moses, Blavatsky, Olcott, Asoka, Jehangir, Akbar, Cicero, Blake, Shelley, Keats, Garibaldi, Huxley, Newton……"a scattering of European scientists ... and philosophers"。

Arundale的修辞充满了temple隐喻：进入者必须脱鞋、保持沉默——"the sanctum sanctorum of the Shrine"。但年访问量仅1000-2000人（相比之下Calcutta Indian Museum年访问量100万），暴露了这种杂交项目的失败。

**L### 3.2.7 Gandhi与Lavater的面相学（1880s）**

> L### "I read Lavater's book and found it more difficult than Snell's Equity and scarcely interesting. I studied Shakespeare's physiognomy, but did not acquire the knack of finding out the Shakespeares walking up and down the streets of London."

Gandhi在伦敦按照Naoroji的建议阅读Lavater但无法从面孔"读出"品格——这一细节成为连接第2章（Arundale的Victorian moral portraiture失败）和第3章（Nagda的vyaktitva/charitra二分）的完美过渡。

## L### 四、逻辑梳理（论证链条+因果转折）

### L### 4.1 总体论证链

```
19世纪印度本土摄影工作室的大量涌现（1850s–）
→ cartes-de-visite的跨文化相似性（"difficult to tell them apart"）
→ Swagger portrait传统对本土工作室的影响
→ Painted photographs的符号学意义（涂料不是污染）
→ 印度宫廷摄影：Lala Deen Dayal, Ram Singh II, Ramchandra Rao & Pratap Rao
→ 摄影作为"person-building"技术（与建筑平行的"augmentation"）
→ D. G. Phalke: 多媒介交汇的历史时刻（摄影+戏剧+chromolithography+电影）
→ Ravi Varma vs. Phalke: mimesis vs. creative intervention
→ Gutman批判："Indian-ness"的本质化谬误
→ "official vs. personal photography"作为替代分析框架
→ Representative Men of India: de-ethnicized elite portraiture
→ Nara Ratna Mandir: darshan与Victorian moral portraiture的冲突
→ Gandhi读Lavater: 面相学在印度语境中的"失败"
```

### L### 4.2 关键因果转折

**L### 4.2.1 从"模仿"到"创造"**

早期印度工作室（1850s–1860s）的产品与欧洲工作室"difficult to tell them apart"——"early Indian photographic practitioners were part of an élite that mimicked key colonial aesthetic forms"。但到了Ram Singh II的时代（1870s），出现了创造性转化——yogic肖像不是对欧洲模式的模仿，而是国王与英国摄影师"jointly constructed"的新的视觉陈述。

**L### 4.2.2 Phalke的转折时刻**

Phalke观看《The Life of Christ》→"I was mentally visualizing the Gods, Shri Krishna, Shri Ramachandra, their Gokul and Ayodhya"——这是印度视觉现代性的一个"birth moment"。Phalke的多媒介学徒经历（J.J. School of Art→学习绘画照片→Ratlam学习三色印刷→魔术师→电影导演）在一个个体身上凝聚了视觉文化的多重转型。

**L### 4.2.3 Nara Ratna Mandir的失败转折**

Palmerston的National Portrait Gallery（伦敦）→Arundale在Indore复制这一模式→添加temple的语汇→两种肖像意识形态的矛盾→失败。这一转折揭示了Victorian moral portraiture在印度语境中的不可移植性。

| 行政报告 | Indore State Administration Reports（Nara Ratna Mandir的访问量数据） |

| 电影史材料 | Phalke的影片（Shree Krishna Janma, Kaliya Mardan）；Rajadhyaksha的研究 |
| 通神学文献 | Blavatsky, Olcott, Leadbeater, Besant的相关文本；Punch的讽刺诗 |

### L### 5.2 材料使用特色

**L### 5.2.1 对比性肖像分析**

Pinney使用Batoni的Portrait of a Gentleman（c. 1760s）作为"swagger portrait"的视觉范式，然后追踪这一范式如何被Bombay的印度工作室本土化——"it is difficult to tell them apart"（L###）。这种跨文化的视觉比较是本章的基本方法。

**L### 5.2.2 建筑与摄影的并置**

Lalbag Palace的巴洛克折衷主义（"French, Italian and German"风格）被作为理解Holkar王室摄影的平行文本——建筑和摄影都是"technologies of augmentation"。

**L### 5.2.3 寓言性轶事的运用**

Phalke的"ghost in the camera"和Ravi Varma的"Chitralekha"被作为两个"寓言"（parable）来解读，将个人的摄影实践提升为关于视觉表征本质的哲学陈述。

## L### 六、论辩与阐述方法

### L### 6.1 核心论辩策略

**L### 6.1.1 Gutman作为"dialectical foil"**

Pinney对Gutman的处理非常精细：不是简单地拒绝，而是"valuable observations to be extracted from the exaggeration and excess"——肯定Gutman对painted photographs的关注，但否定其"hyperbolic essentialization of an Indian alterity"。这种"部分肯定+系统批判"的策略使得Pinney自己的立场更加具有说服力。

**L### 6.1.2 宫廷作为分析空间**

第2章的核心分析空间不是"国家"（第1章）或"村庄/城镇"（第3章），而是"court"——Indore的Holkar宫廷、Jaipur的Rajput宫廷、Hyderabad的Nizam宫廷。宫廷空间的特征是patronage（赞助关系）和performance（表演性），这决定了此处的摄影实践与第1章截然不同。

**L### 6.1.3 概念二分作为分析工具**

- Swagger vs. Protestant portraiture（Wilton）
- Mimesis vs. Creative intervention（Ravi Varma vs. Phalke）
- Darshan vs. Moral physiognomy（Arundale's confusion）
- Official photography vs. Personal photography（vs. Gutman's Indian vs. Western）

这些概念二分不是僵化的框架，而是"启发式"的分析工具，Pinney在应用时保持着历史化的灵活性。

### L### 6.2 阐述风格

- **视觉批判中的移动焦点**：从广告文字到照片到建筑到电影到政治——焦点在不同媒介之间不断移动，反映本章的核心论点：视觉实践的"inter-ocular"性。
- **传记叙事**：Deen Dayal, Ram Singh II, Phalke的职业生涯被作为微观叙事来驱动章节的历史脉络。
- **"theosophical"作为分析维度**：Pinney对Arundale和通神学的处理既不是讽刺也不是赞同，而是一种民族志式的悬置判断——关注通神学的逻辑如何塑造了Nara Ratna Mandir的形式和失败。

## L### 七、语言文风（原文摘录+L###）

### L### 7.1 原文摘录

> L### "Is there a ruling chief or a petty chief in India that has not got an outfit consisting of several cameras and lenses and all the rest of it? In fact, every tenth person you meet is a photographer, or at least carries a camera in his hand, if not with a strap over his shoulder."（Paul S. devLany，开篇引文）

> L### "Looking at early cartes-de-visite and cabinet cards produced by English- and Indian-run studios in Bombay, it is difficult to tell them apart."（L###）

> L### "We are in the realm of theatre – elaborately coded fictions. The silks, satins and taffetas worn by these men and women, the ermine, periwigs and braid that artists painted with such finesse and brio, are not clothes so much as costumes: signals of rank."（Sarah Kent，关于swagger portrait）

> L### "painting on photographs suggests a kind of impurity – on the one hand an excuse for failing to reach more perfect heights, on the other, a device for watering down what should be a purely photographic statement."（Gutman，被Pinney引用）

> L### "The artifice, variety, esoteric and antique allusions in the palace ... suggest a successful baroque attempt to boost royal and civic pride. ... The overbearing impression as we leave Lalbag, is of a gentle colossus with a severe, rather forbidding external presence, concealing a jubilant, ever playful heart within."（Kalyan Kumar Chakravarty，关于Lalbag Palace）

> L### "Just as in architecture king-building involves the accumulation of different national styles, and rooms associated with different places and epochs, so these photographic images suggest a person-building in which different performances are acquired."（Pinney）

> L### "Could we, the sons of India, ever be able to see Indian images on the screen?"（D. G. Phalke）

> L### "True greatness is universal, belongs to all nations, is the property of the world, be its race and its faith what they may."（G. S. Arundale，Nara Ratna Mandir开幕词）

> L### "There was something hybrid and confused about Arundale's intentions, for he sought to collapse local practices of image use with Palmerston's high Victorian notion of moral portraiture."（Pinney）

> L### "I read Lavater's book and found it more difficult than Snell's Equity and scarcely interesting. I studied Shakespeare's physiognomy, but did not acquire the knack of finding out the Shakespeares walking up and down the streets of London."（M. K. Gandhi）

### L### 7.2 文风特征

1. **反讽距离**：Pinney对殖民者和通神学家的书写保持着微妙的讽刺距离。如对Blavatsky的描述——"theosophy, for all its Blavatskian lunacy"；对Punch讽刺诗的引用——"I AM KOOT HOOMIBOOG"。
2. **视觉细节的精微描述**：对painted cartes-de-visite的描写——"Only the faces, hands and feet of the Parsi couple and their daughter have not been overpainted. The studio carpet has been lovingly re-created with minute attention to its floral pattern"——体现了艺术史训练的痕迹。
3. **"jointly constructed"的强调**：反复强调图像的"共同建构"性质——Ram Singh II & T. Murray, Ramchandra Rao & Pratap Rao & Holkars——拒绝单一作者的神话。
4. **Theosophical语言的"对位"**：Pinney让Arundale的宏大修辞（"sanctum sanctorum"、"flowers of greatness"）与其平庸的访问量数据形成对位——这是最有效的批评方式。

## L### 九、与前后章关联

### L### 9.1 与第1章的关联

- **并行/对照**：第1章的"official photography"涉及的是殖民国家权力；第2章的"personal/élite photography"涉及的是印度精英的自我表征。两者共享一套技术，但处于不同的权力场域。
- **关键区分**："what differentiates the images we have seen in the earlier chapter from the cabinet cards and cartes-de-visite ... is, first and foremost, the field of power around the camera rather than cultural practice"（L###）。
- **Indian photographers参与ethnological surveillance**：Pinney指出"Indian photographers were just as capable as Europeans of participating in the project of ethnological surveillance"——这一观察打破了任何简单的二元对立。

### L### 9.2 与第3章的关联

- **Phalke/Ravi Varma→Hindi film→Nagda摄影**：第2章末尾建立的历史链条（Phalke→Ravi Varma Press→chromolithography→Hindi film）直接提供了第3章Nagda摄影的"inter-ocular"前史。
- **Gandhi读Lavater的失败→vyaktitva/charitra二分**：Gandhi无法从面孔"读"出品格——这成为第3章Nagda居民的vyaktitva/charitra区分的历史前奏。
- **Painted photographs的连续**：第2章的painted photographs（Gutman讨论的19世纪实践）在第3章的Nanda Kishor Joshi纪念肖像中找到了20世纪末的回响。
- **"swagger"传统的当代转化**：Batoni的grand tour贵族→Deen Dayal的印度宫廷精英→Nagda照相馆中穿着Rajasthani服装的普通人——"swagger"从阶级特权转化为可购买的摄影服务。

### L### 9.3 与Preface/Prologue的关联

- **"Venetian trade beads"模式在Indore宫廷的回响**：Lalbag Palace的折衷主义建筑（吞噬所有风格）和Ramchandra Rao的飞行/划船照片——都是"cultural coming and going"的实例。
- **"face"/"mask"主题的发展**：第2章中Swagger portrait传统将面孔转化为"costumes: signals of rank"（Kent）——这为第3章的"faces can easily become masks"提供了艺术史的谱系。
