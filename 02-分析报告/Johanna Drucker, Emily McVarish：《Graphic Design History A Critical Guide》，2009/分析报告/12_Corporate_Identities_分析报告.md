# 12. Corporate Identities and International Style (1950s-1970s) 分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

**L001**：本章聚焦战后企业文化的兴起和国际风格的成熟期（1950s-1970s），核心论题是平面设计如何从"设计单个产品"发展到"协调整个组织在多种媒介中的统一形象"——"The work of the graphic designer expanded from composing and stylizing messages to coordinating an 'image' across products and communications."

## 二至三、结构与内容

**L002**：五大板块：Image and identity systems → International style → Style, systems, and graphic design concepts → Technology → The profession。核心案例：

**L003**：**Paul Rand的IBM标志**(1956)："Rand's own identity became a brand." 其设计将"information processing, automated office work, or the scan lines of a monitor"视觉化。**CIBA企业识别系统**(1956-1960)：James K. Fogleman通过颜色、字体和基本视觉元素建立统一形象——"Authority and reliability are expressed by the packaging's consistency." **Unimark International**(1960s)：推广"universal"设计语言——"Ultimately, 'universal' signs succeed by becoming familiar." **William Golden的CBS眼睛标志**(1950)：动态身份系统的先驱。**Cipe Pineles**——"one of few female art directors of her time"——《Seventeen》(1949)杂志。

**L004**：**国际字体排印风格**(International Typographic Style/Swiss Style)：网格系统、无衬线字体(Akzidenz Grotesk, Helvetica, Univers)、非对称布局——将包豪斯/构成主义的遗产提炼为高度系统化的方法论。**Alexey Brodovitch的Portfolio**(1950)——"a bold graphic design experiment that failed commercially"但展示了空前的摄影与设计敏感性。

**L005**：**技术进步**：照相排版（phototypesetting）的出现使得字体设计和排版不再受金属铸造的物理限制——"Photo-based production evolved quickly, offering changing tools for changing tasks."

## 六、论辩方法

**L006**：本章将"identity"（身份/识别）作为核心分析概念——不仅是企业需要"身份"，设计师本人也需要在专业共同体中建构"身份"（通过AIGA、AGI等专业组织）。

## 七、原文摘录

**L007**："Uniformity (conformity) and abstraction were the hallmarks of corporate style." **L008**："Corporate identity and anonymity combine to reinforce the breachless representation of power."
