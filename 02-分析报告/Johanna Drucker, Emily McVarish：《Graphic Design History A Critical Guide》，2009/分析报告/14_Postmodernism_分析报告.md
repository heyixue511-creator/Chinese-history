# 14. Postmodernism in Design (1970s-1980s and Beyond) 分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

**L001**：本章聚焦于1970年代至1980年代及以后的后现代主义设计，在全书中承担着对现代主义"普遍性"叙事的全面反思功能。核心定义："Postmodernism was a deliberate, self-conscious reaction to the 'universal' formal language of modernism that sparked an eclectic interest in historicist, retro, techno, and vernacular styles."

## 二至三、结构与内容

**L002**：五大板块：Postmodern styles → Postmodern consumption and conservatism → Critical theory and postmodern sensibility → Postmodernism and activism → Changes in the profession。

**L003**：核心案例：**April Greiman的《Vertigo》**(1979)——将巴塞尔学到的现代主义规则打破为自由漂浮的形状和色彩。**Wolfgang Weingart**——巴塞尔设计学院的"规则破坏者"，"jamming margins, violating grids, mixing typefaces." **Paula Scher的Swatch海报**(1985)——直接引用Herbert Matter 1934年的瑞士旅游海报，实践了后现代的"引用"美学。**David Carson的《Ray Gun》**(1993)——"creating a different, radically inventive design for each issue...defiant in their disregard for functionality." **Tibor Kalman的M&Co与《Colors》**(1990s)——由Benetton资助，处理种族、AIDS等争议性话题。**Muriel Cooper的《Learning from Las Vegas》**(1972)——将拉斯维加斯的商业符号提升为后现代建筑理论的分析对象，标志了从现代到后现代的转折。

**L004**：**关键理论**：Jean-Francois Lyotard的"grand narratives"批判、Jean Baudrillard的"simulation/simulacra"理论、Guy Debord的"spectacle"——"Images were discussed as simulacra—figures without connection to a source or context."

**L005**：**行动主义设计**：ACT UP的"Silence = Death"(1987)和Gran Fury的公共健康运动——"These information campaigns became models of activist work." Adbusters(1989年创办；源文件未提及创办人姓名，"Kalle Lasn"为外部知识)的"culture jamming"实践。

**L006**：**技术**：Apple Macintosh(1984)使得数字化创作成为可能——"Photoshop filters created effects most designers had only dreamed of prior to the advent of the Apple Macintosh computer." 但早期后现代设计实际上是"a photomechanical production, not an electronic one."

## 七、原文摘录

**L007**："Postmodern design seemed largely a matter of style. But, in fact, postmodern designers questioned assumptions about representation and communication that had been the basis of graphic design since its origins." **L008**："History itself seemed to be constituted by style motifs, a set of forms to be appropriated, rather than studied seriously."
