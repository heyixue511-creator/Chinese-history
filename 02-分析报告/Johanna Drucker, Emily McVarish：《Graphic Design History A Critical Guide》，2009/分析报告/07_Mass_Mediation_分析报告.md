# 7. Mass Mediation (1850-1900s) 分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

**L001**：本章覆盖1850年代至1900年代，核心论题是印刷媒介如何从"一种传播工具"转变为"社会交往的中介基础设施"——"The more that printed matter came to mediate social transactions, values, and communication, the more it was taken for granted as 'natural.'" 这在全书叙事中标志着一个关键转变：设计不仅服务于传播，而且建构了现代生活的日常运作方式。

## 二至三、结构与内容分析

**L002**：六大板块：Printed mass media → Changes in print technology → Media networks → Graphic design and advertising → Posters and public space → Conclusion。核心案例包括：**Linotype/Monotype铸排机**（1884-1885年Ottmar Mergenthaler和Tolbert Lanston的发明）——"the first major innovation in metal type production since Gutenberg, almost four hundred years earlier." **Halftone网屏**（20世纪初完善）——使照片可以和其他图文一起印刷。**Jules Cheret**——提升了石版海报的艺术高度："the ideal height for a woman was eight feet tall—the size of the enormous litho stones used for posters." **Lucien Bernhard的Sachplakat**（物体海报，1908）——"Isolating objects as highly aesthetic commodities in an abstract field removed them from any connection to systems of production."

**L003**：**广告业专业化**：第一美国广告代理公司1841年开业，N.W. Ayer 1869年建立，"Advertising was the first specialty to become fully established as a professional service." **品牌的长寿**——"Coca-Cola, Quaker oats, UNCLE BEN'S rice, and Campbell's soup are among the long-lived brands whose identities were created in the late nineteenth century."

**L004**：**媒体网络**：电报（1840s）→电话（1880s）→留声机→电影（1890s）→无线电——"The telegraph was intimately linked to newspaper activity...turned news into a commodity." **电气化**使标牌、照明和夜间城市景观成为平面设计的新领域。

## 六、论辩方法

**L005**：本章的核心方法是通过"基础设施"（infrastructure）概念将平面设计重新定位——不仅关注视觉风格，更关注印刷品如何成为现代官僚体系、资本主义市场、交通系统和身份建构的不可或缺的组成部分。火车票、纸币（"shinplaster"）、邮票、表格——这些被通常设计史忽视的"卑微"物品——被赋予了理论重要性。

## 七、原文摘录

**L006**："Standardization of activities, environments, and forms became a norm, and coordinations of time and space were regularly represented in grids that governed daily life." **L007**："Print media were sites and agents of the genesis and reproduction of social categories."
