# 11. Public Interest Campaigns and Information Design (1930s-1950s) 分析报告

## 一、章节定位与功能

**L001**：本章覆盖大萧条到二战及战后初期（1930s-1950s），核心论题是平面设计如何服务于公共利益——从WPA海报到战时宣传再到信息设计。本章的关键洞见是："The hidden rhetorical strength of information design lay in the appearance of fact-based objectivity"——信息图表最强大的说服力在于其伪装为"客观事实"的修辞。

## 二至三、结构与内容

**L002**：五大板块：Public interest and education → Photojournalism and documentary → Wartime propaganda → Wartime information → Information analysis and design process。核心案例：

**L003**：**WPA海报计划**(1936-1943)：美国政府资助的公共艺术项目，产出2000多张海报——"providing employment"的同时"acquainted varied audiences with contemporary art." **Otto Neurath的ISOTYPE**(1936)：国际字体图像教育系统——"a universal language, a visual Esperanto"——由Gerd Arntz设计的简化人形符号系统，是今日信息图标设计的先驱。**Ladislav Sutnar的Catalog Design Progress**(1950)：将系统理论引入平面设计——"conceived of the catalogue as a system...a precedent and guide to navigational questions raised by digital interface design." **Herbert Bayer的World Geo-Graphic Atlas**(1953)——将统计信息、地图和图标系统整合为综合性的视觉知识工具。

**L004**：**战时宣传**：Jean Carlu的"Give 'Em Both Barrels"(1941)将工厂工人与战士视觉并列。美国军事情报系统的符号设计为战后信息设计和界面设计提供了基础。**Dorothea Lange/Walker Evans/Margaret Bourke-White的FSA记录摄影**——"documentary photography became a powerful instrument of political and social commentary."

## 六、论辩方法

**L005**：本章的核心方法是将"信息设计"揭示为一种修辞形式（rhetorical form）——看似中立的网格、箭头、流程图实际上构造了特定的世界观和权力关系："Complex issues become simplified as heterogeneity and anomalies disappear."

## 七、原文摘录

**L006**："Information design relied on an appearance of fact-based objectivity to present arguments as if they were simply empirical statements, rather than graphic forms of persuasion."
